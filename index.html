What Postland does

Practice first, then go live. Below is everything Postland can do.

Rehearsal mode

Nothing is sent

A practice run. Postland goes through the whole post and checks the title, thumbnail and captions, but sends nothing to YouTube, LinkedIn or Instagram.

On air mode

Posts go out

The real thing. Postland sends each post to its platform at the time you chose, once. Switch it on when your plan looks right.

Video editing

Pro and Advance Pro

Trim, cut, add text on screen and tidy the sound, right in your browser.

Claude suggestions

Advance Pro

Upload one long video. Claude suggests titles, descriptions, captions and cover words, and picks a 40 to 50 second part for a Short.
